# LegalIR Task 1 Dataset Statement

- **Dataset**: task1_canonical v2
- **Source**: Official UIT Data Science Challenge 2026 Task 1 training and corpus data
- **Corpus**: 8,532 legal documents, 1,153,876 micro/macro chunks
- **Train Queries**: 7,000 legal queries with 7,637 relevance judgments
- **Public Queries**: 1,000 official public test queries
- **Splits**: 5-fold cross-validation and document-disjoint robustness split
- **Duplicate Groups**: 4 duplicate document groups explicitly cataloged
- **Compliance**: Zero external data, zero synthetic data, 100% BTC-authorized
